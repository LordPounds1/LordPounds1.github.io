Fixus Uploader

A personal video-publishing tool for one YouTube channel.

Fixus Uploader is a non-commercial tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It prepares Deadlock gameplay videos from Twitch broadcasts and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel with the YouTube Data API. Every video is uploaded as private; the owner reviews it and decides in YouTube Studio whether to publish it.

Fixus Uploader is not a public service. Only the owner signs in to it, and only to the owner's own channel.

What it does with your Google account

  • Uploads videos and their thumbnails to the signed-in channel (youtube.upload).
  • Reads the channel ID, recent uploads and the status of the videos it uploaded, to avoid duplicates and confirm each upload (youtube.readonly).

It cannot delete or edit existing videos. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
